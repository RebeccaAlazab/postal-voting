During this application, we’ll ask you to upload a photo or scan of your handwritten signature

You might find it easier to apply on a phone or tablet with a camera. It may be quicker to upload the photo to your application there.

If you cannot sign your name, you can ask for a signature waiver during your application.

If you cannot take a photo of your signature now, close this application and try again when you can.

You'll also need:

  • the UK address where you are registered to vote
  • your National Insurance number or other identity documents, for example a passport
